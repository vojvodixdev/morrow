How it's made

From orchard to can in three steps.

  1. 01

    Picked ripe

    Every fruit is picked in its own season, in orchards around Čačak. Nothing is stored for months and pressed later.

  2. 02

    Pressed cold

    Within a day the fruit goes into a hydraulic press. There is no heat, so the juice keeps the taste of the fruit.

  3. 03

    Canned

    The juice goes straight into 330 ml aluminium cans. They keep out light and air, and they recycle again and again.
